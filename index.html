Stall Layout Plotter

True-to-scale site plan: 1 drawing unit = 1 metre. Trace stalls, draw the hall's actual walls, and place columns and doors — measured for pricing and construction reference.

connecting
Pick what to draw — Stall / Wall / Column / Door, next to the mode buttons. Trace a stall — click dots in order, then click the first dot again (or "Close shape") to save it. Trace hall walls — click points along each wall; "Finish wall" ends an open run, or click the first point again to close a loop into a room outline. Drop a column — click where it stands; it's drawn round and to scale. Add a door — click a point on a wall; it snaps to that wall's angle and draws as an open-door swing. Type an exact segment — enter distance + bearing below while tracing a stall or wall. Edit anything — switch to Select, click it to rotate, resize, or delete. Move around — Pan mode to drag the view, scroll or +/− to zoom.
